# Surface: home (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: founder, solo entrepreneur or SME owner deciding whether to email Kevin.
Action: write an email (hello@kevinkrs.com). Proof: experience and education list, ARIC.
Constraints: little text, no theme/gimmick, experience = company/position/location only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, text-first personal site where the person and the facts carry it. Refuses the template stack of hero + three icon cards + stats.
OWN-WORLD: Near-white cool neutral ground (near-black in dark), one ink-blue accent for links and the active state, Geist for everything, hairline rules, no cards, no shadows.
STORY: Visitor learns who Kevin is and what he builds in one glance, scans three offers in one line each, trusts the experience and education lists, then emails.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop two columns. Left, sticky: name large, role, one-line pitch, section nav with a growing-line active indicator, contact icons at the bottom. Right: two-sentence intro, then "What I do". Primary action: email link in the left column, always visible.
FORM: Category canon (standing exit), chosen by the user; references leerob.com, brittanychiang.com. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
