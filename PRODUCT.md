# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), hosted on GitHub Pages at kevinkrs.com. Chosen over plain HTML because a blog will be added later.

## Users

- Solo entrepreneurs and small businesses running their own IT, or figuring out how to use AI properly and get it set up.
- Founders and startups with an idea or early product that needs to become real software.
- SMEs / German Mittelstand that want modern software and sound AI use inside their systems.

They arrive deciding whether Kevin is the right person to hire, often from a referral, LinkedIn or a talk.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Kevin Kraus, freelance Software Engineer in Hamburg. It gets the right clients to make contact. Success means a qualified visitor writes an email.

## Positioning

Kevin combines a strong educational background, real experience shipping products end to end, and agentic engineering in daily practice. He is also an educator: he explains and teaches what he builds (keynotes and workshops, for example with ARIC Hamburg). He builds the software and also gets the team able to work with it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer: turn ideas into working software; help teams scale existing software; establish proper AI use inside clients' systems (agentic pipelines, LLM integrations, team enablement).
- Not offered as a headline service: DevOps (removed by request), generic "backbone"/ramp-up framing (rejected).
- Experience list shows only company, position and location. No years or details; it exists to build trust.
- Single page now; blog to follow later.
- German business site: Impressum and Datenschutz are required.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kevin Kraus. Domain: kevinkrs.com. Contact: hello@kevinkrs.com. LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/kraus-kevin.
- Voice: simple, direct, no buzzwords.

## Evidence on Hand

- ARIC (Artificial Intelligence Center Hamburg): keynotes/workshops, confirmed by Kevin.
- Career stations and education: from Kevin's CV (src/data/site.ts). BCG Platinion, HIVESOUND (1,000+ users, Google Cloud Startup Program), UKE, Celver AG, Uni Hamburg, Yonsei, KIT.
- No photo, logos, testimonials or case studies supplied yet.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with what Kevin builds; AI use is part of building well, not a separate pitch.
2. Simple over clever: few sections, plain language.
3. Trust through evidence (real companies, education, ARIC), never through invented claims.
4. Show the educator side: clarity is part of the product.

## Brand Commitments (visual preference)

- Standing preference: the category standard, played straight. A clean, modern personal site at the craft level of leerob.com and brittanychiang.com. No themed or gimmicky concept (retro desktop, transit lines and similar were rejected).
- Short and scannable: little text.
