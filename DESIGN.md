# Design

Clean, text-first personal site in the category standard, at the bar of leerob.com and brittanychiang.com.

## Layout
- leerob.com-style split. Left: 600px copy column with `@kevinkrs` as the title, a hairline bar (name · role | location), the bio, then sections. Right (≥1024px): a sticky rounded panel with the painted Hamburg harbour scene; hidden on smaller screens.
- Section lists (services, experience, education) are hairline-ruled rows, never cards; hovering a list dims the other rows.

## Color (Restrained)
| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| --bg | #fbfbfc | #0e1013 | page |
| --fg | #16181d | #e8eaee | primary text |
| --muted | #5b616d | #98a0ac | secondary text |
| --rule | #e3e5ea | #24272e | hairlines |
| --accent | #2a5bd7 | #8db0ff | email link, hover, focus |

Light/dark follows the visitor's system setting.

## Type
- Geist Variable, self-hosted via @fontsource. One family for everything.
- Title: clamp(2.2rem, 3.5vw, 2.65rem), 600, -0.02em. Body: 1rem/1.65. Section headings: 1.45rem/600. Row meta: 0.875rem muted.

## Motion
- Only inside the harbour panel (`src/components/Harbor.astro`, generated by `scripts/gen-harbor.py`): drifting clouds, gliding gulls, crossing ship and ferry, shimmering water, swaying trees, grass and flowers. All off under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Rules
- No cards, shadows, gradients, eyebrows or section numbers.
- Short copy. Experience/education rows show only organisation, position, location.
- Content lives in src/data/site.ts.
